AEROPRICE AI · AS-BUILT, NOT CONCEPTUAL

Agentic pricing architecture
for aerospace spare parts

Six specialist agents and one deterministic pricing engine turn a part number into an auditable price recommendation — with a human in the loop whenever policy requires one.

4

Decision gate

Every recommendation leaves the gate on exactly one of two paths.

Within policy

Auto-approved and published to the audit trail immediately — no human touches it.

Margin above floor · change% under threshold · lifecycle not flagged

Flagged

Routed to the Approval Queue for the pricing leader assigned to that part's category.

Margin below floor, or change% exceeds limit, or lifecycle requires review

5

Governance

Who can do what, and what's provable after the fact.

Role-based access

Admin manages policy & users; approver acts only within their assigned categories.

Immutable audit trail

Every decision appended to logs/audit_log.jsonl — never edited in place.

Persisted execution traces

pipeline_runs, agent_executions, tool_executions — real timing & token usage.

Session-based auth

Bcrypt-hashed passwords, server-side sessions, every page requires login.

Scope note: this reflects the system as it exists in this repository today. No RFQ intake, airworthiness/traceability, or market-intelligence agents are implemented — the six agents above are the complete, real pipeline. See ARCHITECTURE.md for the full sequence diagram.
